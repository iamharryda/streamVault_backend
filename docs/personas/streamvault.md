# 🎬 Movie/TV Show Social App

Many movie and TV show enthusiasts want to share their opinions and find recommendations, but current social media platforms are not focused on this need. Reviews are scattered across different services, and engaging in discussions with like-minded users is not always easy.  

We are creating a **social platform** where users can easily share reviews and discover recommendations for TV shows and movies.  
The app will be available as both a **website** and a **mobile application**, providing a dedicated space for movie and TV show fans.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **User Account Creation**: Sign up with an email, choose a username, and set a password.  
- **Content Posting & Contribution**: Add reviews and rate TV shows and movies.  
- **Following System**: Follow friends and others with similar interests.  
- **Movie and Show Selection**: Manually input content or select from a pre-existing database (e.g., [TMDb API](https://www.themoviedb.org/documentation/api)).  
- **Streaming Service Information**: See where movies and TV shows are available for streaming (e.g., Netflix, Viaplay, Disney+, Max).  

---

## 💡 Benefits
- 🎯 Targeted content for movie and TV show enthusiasts  
- 🔎 Easy way to find and give recommendations  
- 👥 Social interaction without the noise of general social media

## 🆚 Competitor Analysis
### IMDb (Internet Movie Database)  
- ✅ Comprehensive database for movies, TV shows, actors, and crew  
- ✅ Users can rate & review  
- ❌ Focus is on information, not community  

### Rotten Tomatoes  
- ✅ Famous for critic & audience “freshness” scores  
- ❌ Limited social interaction  
- ❌ Heavier emphasis on critic reviews  

### Letterboxd  
- ✅ Popular social platform for film enthusiasts  
- ✅ Profiles, lists, ratings, and reviews  
- ❌ Focused only on **movies** (TV shows not yet supported)  
- 📈 Rapidly grew to **17M+ users as of Jan 2025**  

---

## 🚀 How We Stand Out
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Movies **and** TV shows in one place  
- **Community-Oriented**: Follow friends, share opinions, and discuss  
- **Streaming Info**: Quickly find out where to watch (via JustWatch API)

## 🛠️ Development Plan

### **Phase 1 – Core Functionality**
- User Accounts (email registration, login, profile editing)  
- Content Posting (movie & TV show reviews, ratings)  
- Following System (follow/unfollow users)  
- Movie & TV Show Selection (TMDb API integration, manual entry)  
- **Tech stack**:  
  - Frontend → React Native (Expo)  
  - Backend → Node.js + Express  
  - Database → MongoDB Atlas  
  - Hosting → Firebase or AWS  

### **Phase 2 – Enhanced User Experience **
- Streaming Service Information (JustWatch API integration)  
- UI Enhancements (navigation, dark & light mode)  
- Search & Filters (by movies, series, users, reviews)  
- Interactive Features (likes, comments)  
- Profile Customization (avatars, bios, favorites)  
- Multi-language Support (English, Finnish, more)  
- Basic Recommendation System (based on ratings & preferences)  

### **Phase 3 – Future Enhancements **
- Discussion Forums & User Groups  
- Advanced Recommendation System  
- Notifications (likes, comments, new reviews from friends)
